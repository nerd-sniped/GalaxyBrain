# Welcome to GalaxyBrain

You found the `+` — good. This is the hub. Every other note in this garden was hidden behind it until you clicked.

GalaxyBrain is a **starter template** for publishing your own [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) vault as an interactive 3D knowledge graph — exactly like this one.

## What You Just Saw

Every note is a node. Every `[[wikilink]]` is an edge. Notes that only connect through this node collapsed behind it on load, leaving a single starting point instead of a wall of graph.

Click any surrounding node to read it. Right-click to fly the camera there.
