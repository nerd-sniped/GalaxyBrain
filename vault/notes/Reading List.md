## Connecting Themes

Both technical and fiction reading feed the same underlying interest: **how complex systems maintain coherence under pressure**.

- Technical books: distributed systems maintaining consistency under network failures
- Fiction: societies maintaining identity under radical change

See [[Second Brain]] for how these ideas get organised. See [[Philosophy of Mind]] for books on consciousness.
