# Digital Garden

A digital garden is a personal knowledge base that grows and evolves over time — somewhere between a blog and a wiki.

Unlike a blog (reverse-chronological, polished), a garden embraces **work-in-progress notes** openly linked to each other. ^garden-definition

## Core Principles

- **Imperfection is fine** — notes are never "done"
- **Connections matter more than completeness**
- **Writing is thinking** — the act of writing forces clarity

## This Garden

This site is itself a digital garden, built with [[Obsidian]] for authoring and [[Astro]] for publishing.
