# Philosophy of Mind

Philosophy of mind explores the nature of mental phenomena: consciousness, perception, cognition, and identity.

## Hard Problem of Consciousness

David Chalmers coined the "hard problem": why does physical brain activity give rise to *subjective experience*? ^hard-problem

This is distinct from the "easy problems" (explaining attention, memory, behaviour) — which are merely very difficult computationally.

See [[Consciousness]] for more (note not yet written). ^consciousness-ref

## Connection to Digital Gardens

There's a curious parallel: a [[Digital Garden]] is an attempt to *externalise* thought — to make the structure of thinking visible. ^externalisation

This connects to philosophy of mind questions about whether thought is substrate-independent: could a sufficiently rich hyperlinked notes system *be* a kind of distributed cognition?
